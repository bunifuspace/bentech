01

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them.

They allow websites to remember certain information, recognize returning visitors and provide functionality that may improve the browsing experience.

Cookies may be temporary and deleted when you close your browser, or they may remain on your device for a specified period.

02

Why we use cookies

BenTech may use cookies and similar technologies to help operate, secure and improve our website.

  • Keep essential website features working
  • Remember selected preferences
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Improve website performance and usability
  • Maintain website security

The specific cookies used may change as website features and services evolve.

03

Types of cookies

Cookies can generally be grouped according to their purpose and how they operate.

Essential

Required for core website functionality and security.

Preferences

Used to remember settings and choices where supported.

Analytics

May help us understand website usage and performance.

04

Essential cookies

Essential cookies support functions that are necessary for the website to operate correctly.

These may include cookies associated with security, session management, forms or other core functionality.

Because these cookies may be necessary for the website to function, they may not always be available for disabling through the website itself.

05

Preference cookies

Preference cookies may allow a website to remember choices made during previous visits.

Depending on the features available on our website, these may include language, display or other user preferences.

06

Analytics cookies

Where analytics services are implemented, cookies may be used to provide information about how visitors interact with our website.

This information can help us understand which pages are useful, identify performance issues and improve the overall website experience.

Analytics services may collect information such as pages visited, approximate geographic information, device type, browser information and interaction data, depending on the service configuration.

07

Third-party cookies

Some website features may rely on services provided by third parties. Those services may place or access their own cookies or similar technologies.

Examples may include website analytics, embedded media, maps, fonts, security services or other external technologies.

Third-party cookies are controlled by the respective third-party provider and may be subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

08

Managing cookies

Most modern browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies through their privacy or security settings.

You can also configure your browser to notify you when a website attempts to store a cookie.

Blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some parts of the website.

Cookie controls may differ between browsers and devices.

09

BenTech may update this Cookies Policy when our website, technologies or services change.

The date displayed at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.

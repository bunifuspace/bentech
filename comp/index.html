Refunds and warranties.

Clear information about refunds, equipment warranties, installation work and support for BenTech customers.

01

Policy overview

BenTech aims to provide reliable equipment, professional installation and clear customer support.

This policy explains how refunds and warranties may apply to equipment, installation services and other work provided by BenTech.

The specific terms applicable to a project may also be stated in the approved quotation, invoice or service agreement.

02

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the nature of the purchase, whether installation or other services have already been performed, and the terms agreed for the specific project.

Where equipment has not yet been supplied or installed, a refund request may be considered subject to applicable costs and the terms of the relevant quotation or agreement.

Where installation, configuration, consultation or other services have already been performed, charges for completed services may not be refundable.

Approved refunds will normally be processed using a reasonable payment method agreed between BenTech and the customer.

03

Cancellations

Customers should Let's Talk as soon as possible if they wish to cancel or change a project.

Cancellation requests made before equipment procurement or installation may be handled differently from requests made after work has commenced.

Where BenTech has already incurred costs for equipment, materials, transportation, labour or other project requirements, those costs may be considered when processing a cancellation or refund.

04

Special and custom orders

Certain equipment may be ordered specifically for a customer's project and may not be suitable for resale.

Special-order, customized or project-specific equipment may therefore be subject to different cancellation and refund conditions.

Any special conditions will be communicated to the customer before the relevant order is confirmed where reasonably possible.

05

Equipment warranty

Equipment supplied by BenTech may be covered by a manufacturer's warranty where a warranty is provided by the manufacturer or authorized supplier.

Warranty duration and coverage vary depending on the manufacturer, product and supplier.

Where applicable, BenTech may assist customers with the warranty process and communication with the relevant supplier or manufacturer.

Manufacturer warranty

A product warranty does not automatically mean that installation labour, transportation or other service charges are covered.

06

Installation warranty

Where BenTech provides installation services, workmanship may be covered according to the installation terms stated in the applicable quotation, invoice or service agreement.

If an installation-related issue occurs within the applicable workmanship warranty period, BenTech may inspect the issue and determine whether it is related to our installation work.

If the issue is determined to be caused by defective equipment, third-party infrastructure or circumstances outside the installation work, the applicable equipment warranty or service arrangement may apply instead.

07

Warranty exclusions

Warranties may not cover damage, malfunction or failure resulting from circumstances outside the applicable warranty conditions.

  • Improper use or misuse
  • Accidental or physical damage
  • Unauthorized modification or repair
  • Incorrect electrical supply or electrical faults
  • Power surges or unstable power conditions
  • Water, fire or other environmental damage
  • Damage caused by pests or external factors
  • Failure of third-party equipment or infrastructure
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Failure to follow applicable operating instructions

The actual warranty conditions of the relevant manufacturer, supplier or service agreement will determine whether a particular claim is covered.

08

Making a warranty claim

Customers should Let's Talk as soon as a suspected warranty issue is identified.

To help us assess a claim, we may request information such as the customer's name, invoice or project details, equipment information, photographs, videos or a description of the issue.

BenTech may need to inspect the equipment or installation before determining the appropriate resolution.

Depending on the circumstances, the resolution may include troubleshooting, repair, replacement or referral to the applicable manufacturer or supplier.

09

Service and repairs

Repairs or technical support outside an applicable warranty may be provided as a paid service.

Customers will be informed of applicable charges where reasonably possible before non-warranty repair work begins.

Equipment supplied or repaired by a third party may be subject to that provider's own service and warranty terms.

10

Policy limitations

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or restrict any rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable law.

Where the terms of a specific quotation, invoice, manufacturer warranty or service agreement differ from this general policy, the applicable project documentation may govern the particular transaction.

BenTech reserves the right to update this policy when necessary to reflect changes to our services, suppliers or applicable requirements.

11

Contact us

If you have a question about a refund, cancellation, warranty or service issue, please Let's Talk with your project or purchase details.

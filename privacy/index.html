01

Introduction

BenTech respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the BenTech website, contact forms and communications relating to our services.

By using our website or providing information to us, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02

Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with BenTech, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us.

  • Your name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Project or service requirements
  • Information provided through enquiries
  • Information necessary to arrange a site visit or installation

We may also receive basic technical information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device information, approximate location and website usage information, depending on the technologies used on the site.

03

How we use information

Information collected by BenTech may be used to operate our business and provide services to customers.

  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Prepare quotations and project proposals
  • Arrange consultations and site visits
  • Provide installation and support services
  • Communicate about projects and services
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Maintain business and service records
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory requirements

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the reason it was collected unless permitted or required by applicable law.

04

Information sharing

BenTech does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide a requested service, operate our business, comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

For example, information may be provided to relevant suppliers, service providers or technical partners where necessary to fulfil a project or provide an agreed service.

05

Cookies & similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality, remember preferences or understand how visitors interact with the website.

The use of cookies may vary depending on the features implemented on the website.

You can generally control or remove cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

06

Data security

BenTech takes reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Security measures may include appropriate access controls, technical safeguards and administrative practices.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes or satisfy legal obligations.

The retention period may vary depending on the nature of the information and the relationship between you and BenTech.

08

Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information we hold about you.

  • Request information about personal data we hold
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Ask questions about how information is used
  • Request deletion where legally applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

Some requests may be subject to legal, contractual or operational limitations.

09

Third-party services

Our website or communications may use third-party services such as hosting providers, analytics platforms, email services, payment providers or other technical services.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable contractual arrangements.

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. BenTech is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites that we do not operate.

10

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses, organizations, property owners and other customers rather than children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through our website.

11

Changes to this policy

BenTech may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, technology or applicable requirements.

The date displayed at the beginning of this policy indicates when it was most recently updated.

12

Contact us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please Let's Talk.
